# Your intent. Into action.

42-second motion pitch. Source: `apps/web/components/pitch/`. Play at `/pitch`.

The existing acid-lime/ink identity is the foundation. Satoshi carries large
kinetic titles; DM Mono labels actual chapters, constraints and protocol data.
Pink window headers and cyan decision surfaces come from the GGEZ storyboard's
collage vocabulary. The signature: scattered windows resolve into one ordered
protocol action. No video footage, artist imagery or music from GGEZ is reused.

| Time | Story | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | DeFi moves. Make your move. | Oversized type locks into place; forward chevrons cross the frame. |
| 5–10 | One intent. Too many tabs. | Three desktop windows split across the canvas, then converge. |
| 10–17 | Your next move. One desk. | An original desk UI assembles around a 100 USDC swap intent. |
| 17–24 | State in. Decision out. | State becomes four bounded decision fields, revealed together. |
| 24–30 | One desk. Open connections. | The desk connects to Rate and future MCP integrations. |
| 30–36 | Fast decisions. Your permission. | The wallet review panel resolves with explicit spend and slippage limits. |
| 36–42 | Your intent. Into action. | A lime closing slate holds the product promise. |

The pitch demonstrates product direction, not a recorded live transaction. The
Jev scenes and future integrations are labeled as concept. TypeSafe's published
Jev figures are attributed product context: 70–500ms, 40–200× faster
System One-shaped queries, $0.042 per million input tokens, and free output;
these are not eziee runtime measurements. Rate (formerly Iter) is the currently shipped adapter.
The wallet review remains a review, never an automatic confirmation.

The soundtrack is an original synthesized 120 BPM percussion/bass composition.
Web audio starts only after the visitor enables it. The MP4 includes the track.
No voice-over; each scene and caption carries the story without sound.

Playback: play/pause, replay, previous/next chapter, timeline scrubbing, sound,
keyboard Space/Left/Right/Home, and MP4 download. Reduced-motion mode starts
paused, removes animated transforms, and offers static chapter navigation.

Export uses the same React scenes at exact timestamps, captured through
browser-harness at 1920×1080 and 30 fps, then encoded with FFmpeg. The render
bridge exists only with `?render=1`; normal playback doesn't expose it.
